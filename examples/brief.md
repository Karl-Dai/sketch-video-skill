# Example brief: a drop of water

- Audience: curious students; no prior hydrology knowledge.
- Purpose: show that water moves between surface, atmosphere and underground.
- Format: 12-second silent vertical mini-demo; 24 fps. This demonstrates the renderer, not a complete lesson.
- Language: Simplified Chinese.
- Style: warm paper, ink strokes, teal/coral accents; code-drawn diagrams.
- Message: evaporation, condensation and precipitation are part of a larger cycle.
- Boundary: a schematic, not a measured simulation; no claims about residence times or proportions.
- Deliverables: storyboard JSON, H.264 MP4, SRT, narration text, QA frames and verification JSON.
- Evidence: [sources.md](sources.md); no source photographs, fonts or media are included.
- Voice: none. Any later narration needs separate validation and timing review.

For a full lesson, expand the script and durations before rendering; do not squeeze a long script into this mini-demo.
